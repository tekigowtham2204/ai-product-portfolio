## 1. Market Context & Opportunity

### 1.1 Market Size & Growth

The global HR technology market is valued at approximately $35B in 2025, growing at 9.4% CAGR. The AI-powered talent acquisition sub-segment — the most directly relevant category — is valued at $3.2B and growing at 22% CAGR, driven by enterprise automation demand, recruiter productivity pressure, and increasingly sophisticated AI capabilities.

Enterprise companies are under compounding pressure on recruiting efficiency:
- Time-to-fill for specialized roles averages 44 days (up from 38 days in 2023), directly increasing revenue-per-hire costs
- Average cost-per-hire at enterprise organizations exceeds $4,700 (SHRM 2025 data)
- Recruiting team headcount has been reduced at 31% of enterprise companies in 2025, increasing per-recruiter workload

AI tools have been widely adopted in text-based workflows (automated screening, AI-generated interview questions, predictive fit scoring). The gap that no current vendor adequately addresses is **visual workflow integration** — the 35–40% of recruiting-relevant information that exists in visual form (resume images, org charts, whiteboard notes, job description screenshots).

### 1.2 The Multimodal Opportunity

Multimodal AI in enterprise recruiting is an early-stage but high-velocity opportunity:
- No current HR tech vendor offers a production-grade multimodal AI feature for general availability (as of Q1 2026)
- Technical feasibility is confirmed — GPT-4o and Claude 3.5 Sonnet provide production-quality vision inference at enterprise reliability thresholds
- Buyer demand is validated: 8 enterprise RFPs in Q4 2025 specifically included "image input support" as an evaluation criterion

The first vendor to deliver this capability at enterprise quality and compliance standards establishes a durable differentiation advantage, particularly given the high switching costs of enterprise ATS platforms (average contract term: 3 years).

### 1.3 Strategic Framing for HireIQ

HireIQ Vision is not just a feature launch — it is a **platform moment**. The goal is to establish HireIQ as the AI-first recruiting platform that meets users in their actual workflow rather than forcing users to adapt their workflow to the tool's limitations.

The narrative: *"AI recruiting tools have always asked you to translate your work into text first. HireIQ Vision removes that translation layer entirely."*

## 2. Target Segments & Ideal Customer Profile

### 2.1 Primary Segment

**Enterprise Technology & Software Companies (1,000–15,000 employees)**

*Why this segment first:*
- Highest AI adoption rates among enterprise buyers (comfortable with LLM-based tools)
- Most acute recruiter productivity pain (competing for scarce technical talent with high req loads)
- Highest average contract value and expansion potential
- More likely to have internal champions (technical recruiters who already use AI tools)

*Characteristics:*
- Active use of HireIQ platform (≥6 months)
- Recruiting team size ≥8 FTEs
- Average req load ≥8 active reqs per recruiter
- Existing AI tool adoption (indicates AI-receptive culture)

### 2.2 Secondary Segment

**High-Growth Financial Services & FinTech (500–5,000 employees)**

*Why secondary:*
- High recruiting volume with strict compliance requirements — creates both demand and complexity
- Compliance-forward buyers; will push on data handling questions but once satisfied, are high-LTV customers
- SOC 2 and GDPR compliance readiness is critical unlock for this segment

### 2.3 Tertiary Segment (v1.1+ target)

**Healthcare & Life Sciences (1,000–20,000 employees)**

*Why tertiary for now:*
- High potential value (large recruiting teams, significant visual workflow — credentialing documents, org charts)
- Requires HIPAA compliance certification that is not available at GA; planned for Q4 2026

### 2.4 Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

A prospective customer is an ideal fit for HireIQ Vision when all of the following are true:

**Firmographic:**
- Company size: 1,000–15,000 employees
- Industry: Technology, Software, FinTech, Professional Services
- HireIQ platform tier: Enterprise or Growth
- Geography: North America or Western Europe (for data residency clarity)

**Technographic:**
- Already using ≥1 AI tool in recruiting workflow (signals AI-receptive culture)
- Uses cloud-based document management (signals comfort with SaaS data processing)
- Has existing IT/security review process (signals enterprise maturity, not a blocker)

**Behavioral:**
- Recruiter team has ≥8 active requisitions per recruiter per month
- Customer has submitted ≥1 support request or NPS comment related to document/image handling
- Customer has attended an AI-focused HireIQ webinar or event in past 6 months

**Organizational:**
- Has an HR Operations lead or HRIS administrator who can serve as internal champion
- Has executive (CHRO or VP Talent) who has visibility into AI tool adoption ROI

### 2.5 Anti-ICP (Deprioritize for v1.0)

- SMBs (<500 employees): lower per-user value, higher support burden, lower multimodal value due to smaller req volume
- Government/Public Sector: procurement cycles too long for beta cohort; data residency and FedRAMP requirements not met in v1.0
- Healthcare (before HIPAA certification): data handling risk outweighs upside

## 3. Positioning Statement

**For** enterprise HR and recruiting teams who lose hours each week manually transcribing visual candidate information into text-based AI tools,

**HireIQ Vision** is a multimodal AI assistant embedded in the HireIQ platform

**that** processes resume screenshots, org charts, whiteboard interview notes, and job description images directly — without transcription — and delivers structured AI analysis in under 10 seconds.

**Unlike** text-only AI recruiting assistants,

**HireIQ Vision** meets recruiters in their actual workflow, eliminating the translation layer between visual information and AI-powered insight.

## 5. Launch Sequencing

### Gate Criteria

Each phase transition is conditional on the exit criteria defined below, not on calendar date alone. The PM, Eng Lead, and VP Product must collectively sign off on each gate.

### Phase 1: Internal Alpha

**Duration:** Weeks 1–4 (starting [alpha kick-off date])  
**Audience:** HireIQ internal recruiting team + eng dogfood users (~15 users)  
**Goal:** Find and fix critical bugs; establish performance baselines

**Activation Criteria (entry):**
- All P0 functional requirements implemented and unit-tested
- Image upload pipeline deployed to staging environment
- LLM API vendor contract in procurement review

**Exit Criteria (to advance to beta):**
- Zero open P0 bugs
- Image upload success rate ≥97% in internal testing
- Time to first token p95 ≤4 seconds (relaxed threshold)
- Engineering sign-off on image processing pipeline security review
- At least 50 image queries submitted internally with results reviewed

---

### Phase 2: Closed Beta

**Duration:** Weeks 5–10  
**Audience:** 15 enterprise design partner accounts (45 users maximum)  
**Goal:** Validate product-market fit; collect quantitative adoption and quality data; finalize compliance documentation

**Activation Criteria (entry):**
- All Phase 1 exit criteria met
- Beta partner selection complete (see Section 6)
- Legal: DPA with LLM vendor signed; customer DPA addendum ready
- Support team trained on beta feature for Tier 1 handling
- In-product feedback mechanism (thumbs up/down + optional comment) live

**Exit Criteria (to advance to GA):**
- Image query success rate (thumbs-up) ≥75%
- Zero open P0 or P1 bugs
- Time to first token p95 ≤3 seconds
- Net Promoter Score from beta users ≥35
- Legal sign-off on compliance documentation
- At least 3 beta customers provide written case study/testimonial approval
- Sales enablement materials (deck, demo, competitive battlecard) complete
- Help center documentation reviewed and published
- Customer-facing data handling FAQ live

---

### Phase 3: General Availability

**Duration:** Weeks 11–16  
**Audience:** All HireIQ Enterprise + Growth tier customers (Standard tier excluded v1.0)  
**Goal:** Full rollout with controlled ramp, monitoring, and support readiness

**Rollout Cadence:**
- Week 11: 10% canary (randomized within eligible tier)
- Week 12: 25% if Week 11 error rate <0.5% and thumbs-up ≥75%
- Week 13: 50%
- Week 14: 100%
- Weeks 15–16: Monitor, iterate, address GA feedback; prepare v1.1 roadmap

**Activation Criteria (entry):**
- All Phase 2 exit criteria met
- Customer support fully trained and support materials live
- Monitoring dashboards and alerting configured
- Rollback plan documented and rehearsed

**GA Success Criteria (30-day post-launch):**
- Feature adoption: ≥35% of eligible users submit ≥1 image query
- No P0 incidents
- Overall platform NPS does not drop more than 5 points
- Churn rate unchanged

## 6. Beta Rollout Plan

### 6.1 Partner Selection Criteria

Beta partners are selected based on the following scoring model. Candidates with a score ≥70 are invited.

| Criterion | Weight | Scoring Guidance |
|---|---|---|
| Account health (NPS ≥40, no open escalations) | 25% | 0–25 pts |
| ICP fit (firmographic match) | 20% | 0–20 pts |
| AI-forward culture (uses AI tools, CHRO/VP-level AI sponsor) | 20% | 0–20 pts |
| Recruiting volume (≥8 reqs/recruiter/month) | 15% | 0–15 pts |
| Willingness to provide structured feedback (agreed in advance) | 15% | 0–15 pts |
| Geographic/industry diversity (for representative sample) | 5% | 0–5 pts |

**Target Beta Cohort Composition:**
- 8 enterprise tech/software companies (primary ICP)
- 4 FinTech/financial services companies
- 2 professional services companies
- 1 manufacturing/industrial (stretch — to validate non-tech persona)
- Mix: 8 US-based, 5 EU-based (for data residency validation), 2 APAC

### 6.2 Beta Onboarding

**Week 5 (Beta Launch):**
- Kickoff call with each account (45 min): feature demo, feedback expectations, support contacts

**Ongoing:**

- Week 7–8: Async feedback via Slack; Amplitude usage data reviewed weekly
- Week 9: Mid-beta survey (structured 10-question Typeform)

### 6.3 Feedback Cadence

| Method | Frequency | Owner | Audience |

## 7. Post-Launch Metric Review Cadence

### Ongoing Monitoring (Continuous)

- Automated alerts: error rate >0.5%, p95 latency >4s, image upload failure rate >2%
- On-call engineering coverage during GA ramp (weeks 11–14)
- Daily metric digest: adoption, error rate, top error types — auto-sent to PM + Eng slack channel

### Weekly Review (Weeks 11–20)

**Meeting:** 30-minute PM + Eng + ML weekly sync  
**Metrics reviewed:**
- Feature adoption rate (% eligible users, active users)
- Image query success rate (thumbs-up %)
- API performance (latency, error rate)
- Top error categories and resolution status
- Open P0/P1 bug count

### Monthly Executive Review (Ongoing)

**Meeting:** Monthly product review with VP Product, VP Sales, VP CS  
**Metrics reviewed:**
- Primary KPIs vs. target (time-on-task, adoption, quality)
- NPS delta (platform-level and feature-specific)
- Beta-to-GA conversion insights (which features drove value)
- Competitive intelligence updates
- v1.1 roadmap items prioritized by customer demand signals

### Quarterly Business Review

**Metrics reviewed:**
- Contribution to ARR (new ARR attributed to Vision feature in sales process)
- Retention impact (churn rate comparison: Vision users vs. non-users)
- Feature NPS and CSAT
- Comparison vs. success metrics targets (Section 16 of PRD)
- Strategic review: is Vision becoming a table-stakes feature? Adjust differentiation strategy accordingly.

## 9. Pricing Considerations

### 9.1 Pricing Philosophy

HireIQ Vision is positioned as a **platform value driver**, not a standalone upsell. The primary GTM goal for v1.0 is adoption and retention — not incremental revenue extraction. Gating the feature behind additional fees would suppress adoption during the critical market-differentiation window.

### 9.2 Recommended Pricing Structure

**v1.0 (GA):**

| Tier | Multimodal Access | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Enterprise | Included (default on) | Core ICP — want fast adoption |
| Growth | Included (default on) | Capture expansion accounts |
| Standard | Not included | Can be added as upgrade trigger |

**Usage limits (included in tier):**
- Enterprise: 500 image queries/user/month; 10,000/org/month
- Growth: 200 image queries/user/month; 3,000/org/month

**Overage pricing (v1.1+):**
- $0.08 per additional image query beyond tier limit
- Overage alerts at 80% of limit with admin notification

### 9.3 Upsell Path

HireIQ Vision serves as an upgrade trigger for Standard → Growth conversions. Sales playbook:

1. Standard accounts submitting 3+ support requests related to document handling are flagged for growth conversation
2. Outbound sequence: "Did you know HireIQ Vision is available on Growth? Here's a 10-minute demo..."
3. Self-serve upgrade available in admin panel with frictionless trial-to-paid path

### 9.4 Competitive Pricing Note

If Talently AI or another competitor launches a comparable feature at zero additional cost before our GA, we should be prepared to match immediately. Pricing concessions are acceptable in service of adoption velocity during the differentiation window.

## 10. Risk Factors & Mitigations

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| LLM API latency degrades at scale (p95 >4s at high load) | Medium | High | Vendor SLA with financial penalties; fallback to secondary vendor; load testing before GA |
| Multimodal feature enables biased hiring decisions (e.g., AI comments on candidate photos) | Medium | Very High | Explicit system prompt guardrails; EEO advisory in-product; content filtering layer; legal review of system prompt before beta |
| Competitor (Talently AI) launches GA before HireIQ Vision | Low-Medium | Medium | Accelerate beta gate-to-GA timeline if needed; emphasize compliance moat which takes longer to build |
| Enterprise buyers reject image data processing in third-party LLM | Medium | High | DPA signed and prominently available; EU residency option; customer opt-out capability; reference customers in beta cohort |
| Image extraction accuracy underperforms on non-standard resume formats | Medium | Medium | Expanded benchmark test set with diverse formats; quality indicator in responses; beta feedback loop for accuracy improvements |
| Beta partner churn or negative public feedback | Low | Very High | Careful partner selection; dedicated CSM coverage; rapid response SLA for beta issues; no public beta announcement until positive signals confirmed |
| GDPR/EEO regulatory action related to AI-in-hiring | Low | Very High | Legal review at each phase gate; EEO guardrails in model; audit trail for all decisions; policy documentation available to regulators |
| Low adoption post-GA (feature not used) | Low-Medium | Medium | In-product onboarding; CS-led activation campaigns; usage-based alerts to CSMs; feature usage factored into QBR conversations |
| Image content moderation failure (harmful content submitted) | Low | Medium | Content filtering on outputs; rate limiting; abuse reporting mechanism; no user-generated image exposure to other users |
